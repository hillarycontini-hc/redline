INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

This Independent Contractor Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into between Northwind Studio LLC (the “Client”) and the undersigned contractor (the “Contractor”).

1. Services. The Contractor shall provide brand identity and web design services as described in Exhibit A (the “Services”).

2. Compensation. The Client shall pay the Contractor a fixed fee of $6,000 for the Services. Invoices are payable within sixty (60) days of receipt.

3. Ownership of Work Product. All Work Product, including drafts, concepts, and source files, shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Client upon creation, regardless of whether payment has been made.

4. Indemnification. The Contractor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Client and its officers, directors, and employees from and against any and all claims, losses, damages, liabilities, and expenses, including reasonable attorneys’ fees, arising out of or related to the Services.

5. Exclusivity. During the term of this Agreement and for a period of twelve (12) months thereafter, the Contractor shall not provide design services to any business that competes with the Client.

6. Termination. The Client may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or no reason, upon written notice, and shall have no further obligation to the Contractor other than payment for Services accepted prior to the date of termination.

7. Term and Renewal. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.

8. Dispute Resolution. Any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be resolved exclusively by binding arbitration administered by a single arbitrator, and the parties waive any right to bring or participate in a class action.

9. Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior understandings.
